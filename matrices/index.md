Adjacency matrices here are either $\Gamma^{\pm}$ on $4m^2$ vertices, or the descendant graphs on $4m^2-1$ vertices.  
All are in GAP format. Some have additional formatting such as "mat1 := " prior and ";;" following the actual matrix.  

To make sense of these, please see the [preprint](../doc/sankey-srd-II.pdf).
